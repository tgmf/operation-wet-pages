# Operation: Wet Pages — Crew, Tikk & Unnamed NPC Sheets

2026-09-19 · Companion to *Operation Wet Pages — DM Document* and *NPC Character Sheets* · D&D 2024 (5.5e)

## How to use this file

**What this is.** The people the party will bump into who are not on the nine-NPC list: **Tikk** (the music-box operator), the **bosun**, the **helmsman**, the **engineer**, and the **deck sailors** (a template plus six one-line variants, so you can field the DM Document's 4 to 6). Same format as the nine sheets: a full 2024 character at a set level, then a voice-and-habits section. Purrow and his six Resistance sailors are off-ship and not covered.

**Level ladder** (swap any of them; every number below is derived from level, class and scores):

| Level | NPC | Proficiency bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Deck sailors (template) | +2 |
| 3 | Tikk | +2 |
| 4 | Bosun, Engineer | +2 |
| 5 | Helmsman | +3 |

Everyone is below the nine named NPCs and, apart from the helmsman, at or below the party's level 4. That is deliberate: the DM Document calls the crew "commoner" and says class hints are for skill bonuses only. These sheets keep that promise (nobody here fights cats, and the class labels are there so the skill numbers are honest), while giving you real characters if the party gets curious. If you only need a quick number, the At a glance table has AC, HP and best attack.

**Rules reused from the nine-NPC file, in brief.** Cats get Darkvision 60 ft., +1 Dexterity and the free Speedy feat (Speed +10 ft.; its +1 to Dex or Con is already in the scores). Claws are unarmed strikes with Finesse, 1d4 + Str or Dex. Outside a bubble the Filter gives Disadvantage on attacks, damaging spells, Intimidation and any check meant to hurt or coerce a cat, makes cats respond to violence and theft with comic politeness, blinds them to magic, and forces a DC 12 Charisma save within 5 ft of open water; sailors of the Reckless have Advantage on that save. Inside a running bubble none of it applies. Every feature that is magic (spellcasting, Bardic Inspiration, elixirs, Cutting Words, infusions) is listed under a **Bubble only** heading and every cast there risks a surge. Species chassis are borrowed from the 2024 species list (Human here); class features are summarised by name, so check the PHB for exact wording. A dagger (†) marks a mechanic, habit or line invented for this file; strike anything you dislike.

**Decisions made for you.**

- **Tikk's stat line.** The DM Document gives two: "AC 14, 26 HP, does not fight, Stealth +3" (Mechanics 2) and "AC 12, 16 HP, Stealth +3, no attacks, carries the box" (Pre-gens). I used the Mechanics 2 line because it is the rules section and the operator's HP matters when fish and storms arrive. The build hits AC 14 and Stealth +3 exactly. HP comes out at **27**, one above the printed 26: a legal level-3 Artificer with d8 hit dice cannot land on 26. Say "26" at the table if you like the printed number; nobody will notice the point.
- **Speed.** The DM Document gives the operator Speed 30 ft. (half while cranking). The cat package would make it 40, so I treated 30 as his speed *while carrying the box* and 40 as his speed with it set down. Cranking is 15 either way.
- **Marty is Urch.** Recorded as the current surname (the nine-NPC sheet already used it). See Continuity flags for the one knock-on question.

**Voices.** None of these characters speak on the page yet, so every voice here is a first draft extrapolated from the DM Document's ambient beats (the helmsman's three notes, the greasing argument, the lost hat, the engineer's shout, the bosun's headcount). Russian lines in «» are optional flavour, marked †.

## 1 · Tikk (Тикк), the operator

*Level 3 · Artificer (Alchemist) 3 · Human chassis (Small) with cat package · Criminal background · Lawful Neutral*

**AC** 14 (chain shirt 13 + Dex 1; matches the DM Document) **Initiative** +3 (13; Alert adds PB)
**HP** 27 (8 at 1st level, then 5 per level, Con +1, Tough +2 per level; DM Document prints 26)
**Speed** 30 ft. carrying the box (40 ft. without it, 15 ft. while cranking) **Proficiency Bonus** +2

| Ability | Score | Mod | Save | Ability | Score | Mod | Save |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Str | 10 | +0 | +0 | Int | 16 | +3 | +5 |
| Dex | 12 | +1 | +1 | Wis | 12 | +1 | +1 |
| Con | 12 | +1 | +3 | Cha | 8 | −1 | −1 |

**Skills** Insight +3, Investigation +5, Perception +3, Sleight of Hand +3, Stealth +3
**Tools** Thieves' Tools, Tinker's Tools, Alchemist's Supplies
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 13, Passive Insight 13, Passive Investigation 15
**Languages** Common; Resistance hand-signals †
**Feats and ASIs** Alert (Criminal); Skillful (Human: Insight); Tough (Human Versatile); Speedy (cat)

**Class features (Artificer 3).** Tinker's Magic, Replicate Magic Item (inert outside a bubble), Right Tool for the Job. Alchemist: Tool Proficiency, Alchemist Spells, Experimental Elixir. *Alert:* Initiative Swap (he will swap with a PC on request; it is the one thing he offers unprompted).

**Attacks.** *Claws:* +3, reach 5 ft, 3 (1d4 + 1) Slashing damage. He will not use them; see Does Not Fight.

**Bubble only: spellcasting.** Int, spell save DC 13, +5 to hit. Slots: three 1st. Cantrips: Mending, Guidance. Prepared: Cure Wounds, Detect Magic, Sanctuary, Purify Food and Drink, plus Alchemist's always-prepared Healing Word and Ray of Sickness (reflavour as a sudden sour taste; nothing dries or burns). Experimental Elixir: one per long rest. Tikk lives at the centre of the bubble, so his own casting is always legal and always risks a surge (d20 ≤ 5); surge 19 jams his own crank. He will not cast unless a PC asks, and he blanches when they do: "Every cast is a roll against my box, boss."

**Story traits**

***The Operator.*** Carries and cranks the box: 20 ft. radius, magic works only inside, and the bubble stops the moment the crank stops. Any PC may give him one command per turn as a free action ("follow me", "stay", "to the hatch"); he acts on his own initiative after the command, and with no command he keeps doing the last thing. If he is down or separated, a PC may crank instead at the cost of their action each turn. In the storm the waltz is inaudible and cranking on a heaving deck costs him a Str save DC 10 each round (his save is +0, so he loses the box about 45% of the time; that is the point) or the box slides.
***The Marty Rule.*** Standing order: never within 20 ft of Marty. He refuses and says why: "The grey one is off limits, Captain's word." He also refuses magic on Rokko (Captain's order, same as always). This is loyalty, not stubbornness: Persuasion cannot move him. †Escalation: first a polite refusal, second a flat one, third he puts the box down, sits on it, and stops talking until the PC has moved.
***Does Not Fight.*** Filtered cats have Disadvantage against other cats anyway; Tikk simply never tries. Against fish he does one thing: turns his back and shields the box with his body (fish Slap targets the box if one is near him). If a PC needs him to carry, haul or row, he can take over one physical task per run (DM Document: running lethal).
***Filtered Cat.*** Cat template. He is not a sailor of the Reckless, so the water-fear save (DC 12) has no Advantage: teach the Filter on the rowboat with his first roll. †*Braver Inside:* inside the bubble there is no fear roll, so he is calmest while cranking. He has noticed this and will not say why.
***Loyalty Stack †.*** His priorities, in order: Purrow's orders, the box's safety, the PCs' commands. Anything that puts the box within 20 ft of Marty or in the sea loses to the first two.
***Fragile Box.*** The box is AC 10, 5 HP. He hits Dex DC 10 to clear a stuck crank (Complication 6) at +1.

**Gear.** The music box (thin waltz audible at 30 ft., 15 ft. in the hold), spare crank pin, oil can, lantern shutter, chain shirt under an oilskin, a card of hand-signals.

### Voice and habits

**Register and pace.** Young, earnest, clipped. Reports rather than converses: short, factual, in counts. Speaks a beat before he is spoken to and a beat quieter than everyone else. Calls whoever gave the last command "boss", and calls the box "she".

**Verbal tics.** "Cranking." "Bubble's steady." "Twenty feet, boss." "Captain's word." He counts crank turns under his breath ("fifty-one, fifty-two") and loses the count if he is spoken to, then apologises to the box.

**Habits and business.** Oils the crank every ten minutes. Presses the crank pin into his thumb when nervous. Paces the radius with his toes before he cranks. Sits on the box case when idle. Winds the crank a quarter turn without engaging it when he is anxious, then looks guilty. Will not eat while cranking; will eat anything the Cook hands him afterwards without asking what it is. Under the Filter: "Oh, do you need the crank? Take it, take it. (beat) Please give it back."

**Sample lines.** First bubble: "Bubble's up. That's twenty feet, boss, you feel it? Don't wander." Refusal: "Twenty feet, boss. The grey one's off limits. Captain's word. I don't get to know why and neither do you." Storm: "Can't hear her! Cranking anyway!" Caught on deck by the Cook: "I was only holding the box. She's heavy."

**Under pressure.** Goes quiet, counts louder. If the crank jams he will say "sorry, sorry" to the box before he says anything to a PC.

**At the table.** Give him one line a round and never a plan. He is a resource with a temperament, not a companion character: he says yes to almost anything except Marty, the sea and Rokko-magic, and the way he says no should be the same every time so the players learn where the wall is.

**AC** 14 (studded leather 12 + Dex 1 + Defense 1) **Initiative** +1 (11)
**HP** 36 (10 at 1st level, then 6 per level, Con +2)
**Speed** 40 ft. **Proficiency Bonus** +2

| Ability | Score | Mod | Save | Ability | Score | Mod | Save |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Str | 16 | +3 | +5 | Int | 10 | +0 | +0 |
| Dex | 13 | +1 | +1 | Wis | 12 | +1 | +1 |
| Con | 14 | +2 | +4 | Cha | 14 | +2 | +2 |

**Skills** Acrobatics +3, Athletics +5, Intimidation +4, Perception +3, Persuasion +4
**Tools** Navigator's Tools, Carpenter's Tools (Student of War), three musical instruments (bosun's pipe and two more), water vehicles
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 13, Passive Insight 11
**Languages** Common; the pipe's signals †
**Feats and ASIs** Tavern Brawler (Sailor); Skillful (Human: Intimidation); Musician (Human Versatile); Speedy (cat); Inspiring Leader at Fighter 4

**Class features (Fighter 4).** Fighting Style: Defense. Second Wind (3 uses), Weapon Mastery, Action Surge (1), Tactical Mind. Battle Master: Combat Superiority (4d8, save DC 13), Student of War. Maneuvers: Rally, Commander's Strike, Maneuvering Attack (martial, work everywhere; Rally is the one he actually uses). *Inspiring Leader:* a 10-minute talk gives up to six allies temp HP 6 (level 4 + Cha 2), once per rest. *Musician:* his pipe after a rest gives Heroic Inspiration to up to 3 allies.

**Attacks.** *Claws:* +5, reach 5 ft, 5 (1d4 + 3) Slashing damage. *Boat hook* (quarterstaff, Topple): +5, 6 (1d6 + 3) Bludgeoning damage.

**Story traits**

***Sailor of the Reckless.*** Advantage on the water-fear save.
***The Orbit.*** Bow, midship, stern, roughly once a segment through Act I, chivvying the deck crew. From P1 (all hands to the winch) he stays at the winch, Location 2, and pulls with the crew until the cable goes wrong in W2. †Reconcile with the DM Document's "joins the winch effort the moment descent prep starts": read "prep" as the P1 order.
***The Chivvy.*** His Persuasion +4 works on crew and PCs. His Intimidation +4 has Disadvantage against cats (the Filter), so what comes out is a gentle, exasperated request; he has never noticed that this is why nobody hurries. Against fish there is no Filter, and he is suddenly very loud.
***The Headcount †.*** In W3 he counts aloud, twice, and gets a different number. The rule of thumb: the first count skips himself, the second includes him, and Kot Kong counts as two. If a PC is on deck at dusk looking busy, he counts them as crew (no roll) and blames his arithmetic later; a stowaway who is hiding indoors changes nothing. Do not let the headcount become a puzzle to solve: it is a joke that also protects the party.
***The Pipe.*** One long blast: gather. Two short: winch. Three short: lifeboats. A PC who studies it (Investigation DC 10, or one listen) knows the signals; a PC who blows the wrong one causes 1d4 rounds of comic confusion and a Suspicion tick if the Captain sees.
***Filtered Cat.*** Cat template.

**Gear.** Bosun's pipe on a lanyard, boat hook, wet tally-book with the crew list (wrong), studded leather under oilskins, a pencil he licks.

### Voice and habits

**Register and pace.** Meant to bark, delivers a polite request. Nautical idiom, brisk, but every command ends in a "please" or a "there's a lad". Slows for numbers and hurries for names.

**Verbal tics.** "Right, lads, if you'd be so kind—". "Heave— heave, and thank you." "That's the ticket." «Ну-ка, дружно!» † when the crew pulls together. Counts by pointing with the pipe.

**Habits and business.** Taps the pipe against his palm before speaking. Licks the pencil to tally. Never sits on deck. Salutes the Captain's chair even when the Captain is not in it. Corrects the crew's coiling of a line without stopping his walk.

**Sample lines.** To sailors: "Mind the— there. Thank you, that's the ticket." To a PC on deck: "You. Yes, you. Would you mind terribly taking the other end?" Headcount: "Eleven. No. Twelve. Hold on, I said—". A robbed bosun: "Oh, did you want the pipe? By all means. (beat) I'll be needing it back for the storm."

**Under pressure.** Louder in the fish fight, quieter in the empty-hook silence. In W3 he keeps counting because it is the only thing he can do.

**At the table.** He is the crew's clock. Use him to move the crew in the direction the segment needs; he should never be a barrier a PC has to roll against unless a PC makes him a reason.

**Feats and ASIs** Tavern Brawler (Sailor); Skillful (Human: Medicine); Musician (Human Versatile); Speedy (cat); Cha +2 at Bard 4

**Attacks.** *Claws:* +5, reach 5 ft, 4 (1d4 + 2) Slashing damage.

Prepared: Cure Wounds, Healing Word, Dissonant Whispers, Tasha's Hideous Laughter, Suggestion, Calm Emotions, Enhance Ability, Hypnotic Pattern, Tongues. Bardic Inspiration (d8, 4 uses, back on a short rest), Cutting Words (reaction). He has never cast knowingly.

**Story traits**

***Sailor of the Reckless.*** Advantage on the water-fear save.
***The Wheel.*** Always at Location 3, the stern, rain or shine; never patrols, never leaves; the single most predictable body on the ship. The ship is at anchor, so he steers nothing, and holds the wheel anyway "to keep her honest". Only the Captain relieves him. A PC can get a conversation (Persuasion DC 12) but never a departure; he talks over his shoulder without turning his head.
***Watching Forward.*** Passive Perception 15 matches the DM Document's Stealth DC 15 "vs sailor" at the stern ladder (Route 1 alternative): the ladder is behind him, and he is looking at the bow.
***Three Notes.*** In P2 he hums the same three notes of a work-song for the whole segment. After a minute nobody on deck notices. A PC who tries to place it (Performance or Insight DC 12) learns only that it is a sea-song "older than the boat". †*Optional hook, yours to keep or strike:* the three notes are the first three of the box's waltz. Nobody knows why.
***Bubble Note †.*** If a bubble runs within 30 ft. of the wheel he notices it (Filter jammed) and keeps it. Roll a d20 at the start of each round: on a 1, the hum lands as a Bardic Inspiration die on the nearest creature that is not him. He is a Suspicion problem and a strange gift at once.
***Steady in the Storm.*** In W2, when the ship heels, he keeps the head to sea and does not fall; nearby creatures have Advantage on saves against being knocked prone while they hold the wheel rail.
***Filtered Cat.*** Cat template.

**Gear.** Leather coat, brass wheel-glove, pennywhistle, tin cup of tea on the binnacle that never spills.

### Voice and habits

**Register and pace.** Slow, warm, baritone. Short statements delivered to the horizon, not to the person; addresses everyone as "friend". Never finishes a sentence he is about to be interrupted in.

**Verbal tics.** "Steady as she goes." "Nothing to see, friend, and a lot of it." «Штурвал не спрашивает.» † ("the wheel doesn't ask"). Answers a question with the answer to the question before it.

**Habits and business.** Hums. Taps a spoke in time. Adjusts the binnacle by a hair, then puts it back. Never sits. Holds the tea level through everything and lifts it to the Captain's chair without looking at it.

**Sample lines.** To a PC arriving at the stern: "Fine evening, friend. Mind the ladder." Asked about the tune: "It was here when I came aboard." In the storm: "She'll hold. Ask me again when she doesn't."

**Under pressure.** Hums lower. If the tune stops, something is wrong.

**At the table.** He is a landmark and a gentle clock. His job is to be where he always is, so a repeat run can plan around him.

Arcane Armor and Guardian's Thunder Gauntlets and Defensive Field are all magic; he wears the suit without ever knowing what it is.

***Below Decks.*** Location 12, the hold and engine room. He is essentially unseen except at watch changes: once pre-descent and once mid-storm (W2). For stealth purposes the engine room is empty the rest of the time. Hot and noisy; the noise masks a music box (audible radius 15 ft. here).
***"The Diesel's Fine."*** In W2 he surfaces just long enough to shout it, then vanishes again.
***Deaf as a Post †.*** Disadvantage on hearing-based Perception checks; immune to the waltz's pull ("cats who hear it come to look"); talks at volume.
***Sabotage Response.*** He is fixing an engine, not hunting a saboteur. A crude jam (Dex DC 14, no tools) stalls it loudly and he arrives at the next watch change. A throttle or a fake fault that "reads as wear" (Artificer or Sailor with a success) is "fixed" by him and costs an hour; a passive Investigation of 16 means he catches a botched forgery. He blames the diesel first, the fuel second, himself third.
***Sailor of the Reckless.*** Advantage on the water-fear save; he never sees open water.
***Filtered Cat.*** Cat template.

**Gear.** Ring-mail boiler-suit, a rag, wrenches, a spare fuel filter, a tin mug of something black.

### Voice and habits

**Register and pace.** Shouted, short, technical. Talks to the engine more than to the crew. Never says more than four words to a stranger.

**Verbal tics.** "DIESEL'S FINE!" «Дизель в порядке!» † "Not the pump. Never the pump." "Who touched the—? Nobody. Good."

**Habits and business.** Pats the block. Listens to it with a screwdriver against his ear. Wipes his paws on the rag and then on the wall. Leaves a warm mug on every ledge he passes.

**Sample lines.** In W2: "DIESEL'S FINE! (to the engine) Aren't you." At a watch change, to a PC: "You. Passing? Mind the belt." After a sabotage: "Fuel. Probably the fuel."

**Under pressure.** Quieter and lower. When the cable goes he is the one who says "that's not the winch" and is right.

**At the table.** Give him two lines a run. He is the reason a plan to jam the engine costs time, not the reason it fails.

### Voice and habits (all sailors)

**Register and pace.** Gracious, curious, gossipy, a little too quick to offer a hand. Superstitious about water in a way that is friendly and specific. Speak in short, cheerful sentences and finish each other's.

**Verbal tics.** "Oh, do you need that? Take it, take it." "Well, I never." "Bless the tide" or "knock on wood" after any mention of the sea. Nobody says "drown"; they say "the D-word" or "going for a long swim".

**Habits and business.** Touch the rail on the way past. Spit over the left shoulder at a gull. Won't whistle on deck (the helmsman is the exception; nobody tells him). Trade small favours for gossip.

**Sample lines.** Gossip table (d6): 1: "The doctor stands at the rail when it rains, poor thing. The grey one brings her tea." 2: "The Cook knows where everything is. *Everything.*" 3: "The Captain has a book. He writes things in it. Don't ask what." 4: "Kot Kong sleeps on the hatch. If you need the hold, ask him nicely." 5: "The little one in the sailor suit takes messages and never delivers the same one twice." 6: "Somebody's been feeding the stray in the hold. We're not supposed to know."

**Under pressure.** In the fish fight they get loud and very good at it. In the empty-hook silence they take their caps off.

**At the table.** They are the ship's texture. Give every one a name only if a PC asks; treat the six variants as a menu, not a cast.
