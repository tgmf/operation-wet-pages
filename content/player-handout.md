# Operation: Wet Pages — Player Handout and Pre-gens

## Player handout — what you know before you roll

Send this as is. It contains only what a low-level operative would know; everything else comes out at the table.

**The world.** Cats fear water. Nobody fights, nobody argues seriously, nobody talks about magic — not in life, not in books. Everyone thinks that is just how cats are. You know better: there is something pressing on every cat's mind, the organisation calls it **the Filter**, and it can be jammed. A hand-cranked music box makes a 20 ft bubble where the Filter stops. Inside it magic works, tempers work, and cats remember what they see. Outside it you will feel the pull toward politeness like everyone else. Magic is real, rare, and you have used it perhaps a dozen times in your life — always inside a bubble, always with a surprise or two.

**The Resistance.** A secret network of cats with "features". You know there are Leaders; you have never met them and don't know how big it all is. You know your cell: **Captain Jack Purrow**, his ship, and the pirate colours you sail under — pirates are the cover, not the job. You have done at least one job with Purrow before. The organisation's purpose, as far as you were told: find out who is doing this to cats, and help the gifted ones before someone else finds them.

**The bigger picture.** This operation is part of a bigger Mission, and the Mission got complicated when Lunna — the rogue operative you were sent to recover — slipped away from you in Livanu and, at the last moment, jumped aboard the *Reckless*. You know that ship. You know its crew: it has been the object of half a dozen surveillance jobs in the last couple of months, some of them yours. A one-legged captain who is not afraid of water. A scientist who found something on the seabed with a sonar. A grey cat with glasses who listens more than he talks, and a red one who never stops talking. A cook, a cabin boy in a sailor suit, a girl with potted plants, and a cat the size of a lifeboat. You don't know why exactly, but these cats and whatever they are doing matter to the Resistance a great deal — the rumour is that the Leaders themselves are personally invested in it. And now Lunna — the Leaders' only daughter, as far as anyone knows — has, to all appearances, defected to that eclectic crew. Nobody up the chain is saying the word "defected". Purrow is saying "recover". You lost her in Livanu; you are the ones being sent to get her back. And the order about the grey cat came from above Purrow — he didn't argue with it, and neither will you.

**The job.** A research ship called the **Reckless** is anchored over something at the bottom of the sea near Livanu. Purrow's orders: bring back **Lunna** — one of ours, aboard as a journalist, and nobody knows if she went willingly; disrupt whatever they are doing down there without anyone learning who you are; and take the scientist's research — copy it if you can, take it if you must. One more: the **grey cat with the glasses** is off limits to the device. No explanation given. The fourth member of your team is **Tikk**, who carries and cranks the box and does what you say.

**Character creation (D&D 2024 rules, level 4).** Roll or point-buy, your call. At level 4 take a feat rather than an ASI — it's more fun here: Actor, Skulker, Lucky, Skilled, Observant, Grappler, Chef, Alert, Telepathic, Telekinetic, Magic Initiate all fit; Fey-Touched is out (no teleporting), and any magical feat works only inside the bubble. Any species, as long as you can describe it as a cat: you get Darkvision 60 ft if you lack it, +1 Dexterity (max 20) and the Speedy feat free. Any class. If you cast: fire and lightning don't exist here — reflavour as cold, water or stone, or pick another spell; every spell needs the bubble and every spell can surge. Attacks against cats are at disadvantage everywhere outside a bubble — build for a heist, not a fight, and bring one sneaky or talky cat and one caster if you can. Backgrounds that fit: Sailor, Criminal, Charlatan, Entertainer, Guide.

## Pre-gens to build

**Pre-gens (level 4, D&D 2024, all cats by the species rule, one feat each on top of the free Speedy):** five sheets so two unprepared players can pick a pair that covers each other. Each is a separate build task.

| # | Concept | Class / subclass | Covers | Signature move in this session |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The climber | Rogue (Thief) · Skulker | Locks, stealth, the cabins | Fast Hands on the chest while the Cook is on the stairs |
| 2 | The talker | Bard (College of Lore) · Actor | Every social route; mind magic | Suggestion inside a bubble, Cutting Words on a fish |
| 3 | The water-witch | Druid (Circle of the Sea) · Lucky | Water magic, fog, the boat | Fog Cloud from the rowboat; Thunderwave reflavoured as a wave |
| 4 | The muscle | Fighter (Battle Master) · Grappler | The fish, the winch, holding Marty | Pushing Attack on the sailfish; Athletics on the rail |
| 5 | The dreamer | Wizard (Illusionist) · Telepathic | Mind magic, distraction | Minor Illusion of the waltz elsewhere; Sleep on the helmsman |

Plus one sheet for **Tikk** (operator NPC): AC 12, 26 HP, Stealth +3, no attacks, carries the box.
