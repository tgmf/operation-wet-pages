## 1. Overview

**Format.** 3–4 hours, D&D 2024, level 4 (a feat each, no 3rd-level spells). Three players, or four with Tikk as a PC.

**Situation.** The Reckless is anchored over the sonar anomaly on its first Catlantis expedition. Lunna, Marty's sister, is aboard as a "journalist fleeing pirates" whom Rokko rescued in Livanu. She already refused retrieval there. Captain Jack Purrow's cell ("pirates" is their cover) tails the Reckless on the Slipping Floppy and sends the team: the PCs and Tikk with the music box.

**Purrow's briefing (hand out):**

1. Find Lunna and bring her back. We don't know if she boarded on her own or is being held.
2. Take Agatha's research. Copy it if you can, take it if you must.
3. Disrupt whatever they are doing down there — without anyone learning who you are.
4. Bonus: if the scientist or anyone else *gifted* wants to come along, bring them. Kidnap only if you're compromised or the papers are lost.
5. Keep the device away from the grey cat with the glasses. That's an order, not a suggestion.

He also gives them the Plan (Act I).

**DM only.** The Resistance thinks the Glowing Cats (SC) steer Marty, Rokko and Agatha, and that revealing itself would make Marty a bigger target. Marty's parents want him out of it for now. Purrow knows a little more than the PCs. Lunna knows far more, including that nobody on the team can promise her anything.

| Goal | Full | Partial | Fail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Documents | Copied with the false pages flagged, originals untouched | Stolen, or copied unflagged | Nothing, or Agatha destroys them |
| 2 · Lunna | Leaves with the team, Marty none the wiser | An agreement: a deal or a controlled channel | Marty learns who she is, or anyone is hurt |
| 3 · Recruits (bonus, repeatable) | Each gifted cat recruited: Litta, Agatha (or her influence broken) | Agreement to stay in touch, or taken by force | Agatha over the rail (she survives; the SC own her); Litta left aboard knowing too much |

Delaying the descent is not a goal; it wins time (section 10).

**Inviolable rules**

- Marty and Rokko are never harmed. If either dies, the run resets.
- The descent happens and Rokko does not come back. Disruption delays it; nothing prevents it.
- No NPC dies. Fish are fair game.
- The box never runs within 20 ft of Marty. Tikk refuses: "The grey one is off limits, Captain's word." With Tikk as a PC, agree this with his player before the session (*Tikk PC Sheet*, The Marty Order).
- PCs can die. A TPK ends the run, not the session.
- Rokko inside a running bubble: see [Rokko and the bubble](#rokko-and-the-bubble).

**Acts:** I Boarding → II Heist (two goals, recruits) → Interlude (flying fish) → III Storm → Conclusion.

## 2. The dream frame

- The team is lying in trance on the Floppy. Two divination wizards run the whole mission as a shared vision. Play it straight; the stakes inside are real.
- **A run ends** on a TPK, a PC gone for good (they wake the others), a dispel, cover blown past saving, or the end of the window (see [Conclusion](#13-conclusion)).
- **The first ending is the reveal.** Steer Run 1 to it. Cut away: two presences are briefly in the dark with the party, watching from far away. The party wakes in the dreamchamber on the Floppy, with Purrow standing over them like this has happened before. Let them ask questions; explain as much as you like.
- **Looping back.** They re-enter from any point of the story they have already seen. Everything resets: locks, Lunna undecided, Agatha's blackout forgotten, every clock to zero. Only what the players know carries over. No items, no Inspiration.
- **Optional timer.** 10 minutes per segment: 60 minutes per run, plus 10 per delay. At zero the dream ends wherever they are.
- Rokko's storm forecast is secretly dream magic (the dragon, the future). Never explain it at the table.

### Rokko and the bubble

If Rokko ends up inside a running bubble, he stops, looks around and says something like *"You guys aren't really here, are you?"* Everyone inside the bubble vanishes for anyone watching from outside.

1. Message the players who were inside, privately. Tell them the truth: the dreamchamber, all of it. Ask them to keep it quiet.
2. Give them the choice: wake everyone now (the run ends, reveal and all) or keep playing on what they know.
3. Tikk as a PC is inside every bubble, so his player is part of every such reveal.

Best when it catches part of the party. If it catches everyone, do the reveal out loud.

## 3. Clocks and complications

All clocks are visible to the players. A run is six segments (beats) of about two hours of ship time, plus one segment per delay. Act I is unclocked.

| Clock | Segments | Advances when | At full |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Descent** ("time left for the Plan") | 6 | Every two hours of ship time. Rokko's announcement strikes 3 at once. Each delay adds one back (no cap) | Rokko goes down. Flip to the Window |
| **Window** | 3: W1 Sky & Rain, W2 Waves & the Cable, W3 Empty Hook & Calm | One segment each | The cable comes up cut, Rokko is lost, Purrow must stand off. The Commit check ([Conclusion](#13-conclusion)) |
| **Suspicion** (crew) | 6 | A PC is caught in a lie or acts hostile toward a cat (approach costs in Act I, robbing a gracious cat, a lie Rokko catches). Anything else only when suspicious in context: the Captain learns the PCs keep working Yanni for keys and parts; cats see Litta cast inside the bubble | The Captain asks the PCs, kindly, to explain themselves in the chart room, with Marty present. Cover is rebuilt or the job goes loud |
| **Lunna's determination** | 4, starts at 1 | The per-beat pushes and pulls in Goal 2 | Entering beat 5 at 3–4 she says the name; Goal 2 blown |
| **Agatha's influence** | 5, starts at 2 | A failed Cha save on a trigger (Goal 3: Agatha) | The rail (Goal 3: Agatha) |

- **Any full clock means a goal fails**, so there is no Commit. The players learn what each full clock means.
- **The Captain's ledger** has no clock. He records what the PCs do and acts, calmly and completely, when a crew member is hurt or the ship endangered.

**Complication deck** (draw when a scene stalls or a roll fails hard):

1. The Captain appears in his chair, mid-sentence, exactly where the PCs are.
2. Yanni arrives with tea and stays.
3. Rokko sits down, smiles: "You're worried about the same thing she is" (nods toward Lunna).
4. The Cook is already in the room, peeling something.
5. Rain: Agatha's trigger. She stands up mid-conversation.
6. The box's crank sticks: Dex DC 10 or the bubble drops for a round.
7. A sailor has found the rowboat and is proudly hauling it aboard "so it doesn't drift away".
8. Marty asks a PC, politely, whether they'd like to talk about it.

**Three PCs:** Tikk (NPC) can take over one physical task once (rowing, hauling, carrying). Purrow's crew appears once on signal. **Four PCs:** add two flying fish to the interlude. Give each player a **cover card** (who they are aboard) and an **ally card** (the NPC they recruited); let them trade.

## 4. Core rules

Teach in this order: species and magic limits (before the session) → the Filter (first contact with crew) → the music box (first time a PC wants to cast) → wild surge (first cast).

### The Filter

Active everywhere except inside a music-box bubble. Affects cats only: not the Captain (a marsupial), not fish.

| Effect | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Aggression suppressed | Disadvantage on attacks, damaging spells, Intimidation and any check to hurt or coerce a cat. PCs and NPCs alike. Not against fish |
| Graciousness | Cats answer violence and theft with comic politeness ("Oh — do you need that? Take it, take it"). They still remember, and grow puzzled |
| Magic blindness | Cats can't perceive or retain magic; a witnessed spell is rationalised within a round ("the lamp flickered"). Inside a bubble they keep it |
| Fear of water | A cat within 5 ft of open water: Cha save DC 12 or spends the turn moving away. Reckless sailors have advantage. The Captain is immune. No roll inside a bubble |
| The worm of doubt | Once per session a PC may spend Inspiration to ignore the Filter for one roll |

- **Inside a bubble:** no disadvantage; cats react to violence like real people (fear, anger, alarm) and remember. A fight inside the bubble is loud and kept.
- **Dream magic** ignores the Filter: no bubble needed, no surge.

**Below the main deck** the Filter thins (cabin deck a little, lower deck more). Spells cast without the box leak through: they cost the slot, never surge, are never noticed, and damage spells do nothing.

- Pass without Trace: +3 on the cabin deck, +4 on the lower deck. Healing: 1 HP. Guidance: +1. Anything else: a tiny, subtle effect.
- One concentration spell can be held on leaked magic.
- Knock and Dispel Magic work on the Captain's safe down here (Goal 1).
- Litta's plants grow faster here. Agatha's saves against Influence improve (Goal 3: Agatha).

### The music box

- **Bubble:** 20 ft radius around the box. Magic works only inside it. Stops the moment the crank stops.
- **Waltz:** audible at 30 ft (15 ft in the hold), inaudible in the storm. Cats who hear it come to look, politely.
- **Fragile:** AC 10, 5 HP. A damaged spare is hidden in Lunna's cabin.
- **Leaving the bubble:** a running effect keeps running, except that a mind effect on a cat ends when that cat leaves the bubble, and concentration ends when the caster is outside a bubble (walked out, or it dropped around them). Leaked magic below decks is the exception.
- Agatha's SC influence is jammed inside a bubble.
- **Tikk as a PC:** cranking is his free object interaction each turn; the bubble lasts until the start of his next turn. Otherwise he plays like any PC. Out of combat he cranks as long as he likes; if he was cranking when Initiative is rolled, the bubble holds until his first turn ends. It drops if he skips a crank, is Incapacitated or loses the box. Any other object interaction that turn costs his Utilize action.
- **Tikk as an NPC:** speed 30 ft, 15 ft while cranking. Any PC may give him one free command per turn; he acts on his own initiative after it. No command: he keeps doing the last thing. AC 12, 26 HP, doesn't fight, Stealth +3. Loyal to Purrow's orders over the PCs' wishes.
- **Anyone else cranking** spends their action each turn.

### Wild surge

After every spell cast inside the bubble the caster rolls d20. On 5 or lower, roll d20 on this table.

| d20 | Surge |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Water: every liquid within 20 ft leaps 5 ft up, then falls. Anyone holding a cup or lantern: Dex save DC 12 or drop it |
| 2 | Water: a wave slaps the nearest porthole or rail; 1d4 cold to the caster, who is soaked (disadvantage on Stealth for 10 minutes) |
| 3 | Water: dense fog fills the bubble for 1 minute (heavily obscured) |
| 4 | Water: a live, furious flying fish drops into the bubble. It Slaps the nearest cat (Dex save DC 12 or drop one held item) and flops for a round. The Cook wants it. After the interlude, it's two fish |
| 5 | Earth: wooden surfaces within 10 ft sprout leaves for 1 minute. Litta notices from anywhere on the ship |
| 6 | Earth: a plank goes soft; each creature in the bubble makes a Dex save DC 12 or falls prone |
| 7 | Earth: the caster's claws grow 1 inch for an hour (+1 unarmed strikes, −1 Sleight of Hand) |
| 8 | Earth: a potted plant, a crate of turnips or the Cook's onions within 20 ft doubles in size |
| 9 | Mind: the caster hears every thought within 20 ft for 1 round. Read one out loud |
| 10 | Mind: the nearest other cat falls asleep for 1 round (no save) |
| 11 | Mind: for 1 minute the caster sees other cats' dreams above their heads. Rokko's is always the sea |
| 12 | Mind: the caster forgets the last minute (the player still knows) |
| 13 | The waltz doubles in volume for 1 minute (audible at 60 ft) |
| 14 | Bubble radius halves for 1 minute |
| 15 | Bubble radius doubles for 1 round — check who is inside now |
| 16 | The caster glows faintly for 1 minute. The Glowing Cats notice: SC Influence +1 on the caster |
| 17 | Echo: next round the same spell fires again from the caster, at a target of the DM's choice |
| 18 | Refund: the slot is not spent |
| 19 | The crank jams for 1 round; the bubble drops. If a concentration spell is running, reroll |
| 20 | Clean cast; the caster feels the Filter part like a curtain: advantage on the next roll of any kind |

### Magic and species

- **Magic:** any spell, but fire and lightning damage don't exist. Reflavour as cold or bludgeoning, or pick another spell.
- **Species:** anything the player can describe as a cat. Add Darkvision 60 ft if missing, and the Speedy feat.

- **Main deck:** open and watched.

- **Lower deck:** hold and engine room; where stowaways hide.

## 7. Act I: Boarding

About 10 minutes.

**The Plan (Purrow's order):** *At dawn, while the crew readies the Alvin, we hail them under pirate colours. You three go aboard as envoys to negotiate a toll. During the parley Tikk and whoever is free search the cabins for the research. Lunna is our hostage — take her as the price of passage. Foul the winch if you get the chance.* Purrow presents it as simply a good plan. The players may follow it, bend it or ignore it. Rokko ruins it at supper.

**Start:** in the rowboat 200 ft off the stern with the PCs, Tikk, one box and the lantern code. Teach water-fear here (first save) and graciousness on first contact.

**Approaches:**

- **Up the hull:** anchor chain (bow) or stern ladder: Athletics DC 14, Stealth DC 15 vs the sailor on watch, water-fear save on the chain. Rigging: Athletics DC 12. Fog or water magic from the rowboat: Stealth DC 12 in fog, plus the surge roll.
- **Stowaways:** the Cook finds them within the hour. His bargain: silence for galley help, which means tray access to every cabin.
- **Castaways:** the Captain and Kot Kong pull them out (Con save vs cold water). Failed Deception (DC 12 as castaways, DC 14 on forged papers): the Captain knows, says nothing, ledger mark.
- **Forged papers vs Marty:** Deception DC 16 vs Insight +6. He may invite a PC for a private talk.
- **Pirate hail:** Persuasion or Performance DC 12 to pass as envoys. The crew are gracious; the Captain unbothered. Lunna recognises the cell's signals and goes cold.
- **Lunna's porthole:** she lowers a rope only for a written note worth answering (Persuasion DC 14, carried by Yanni), or if the team hesitates in the boat too long.
- **Rokko at supper:** reads their nerves ("You're not shipwrecked, you're *excited*"). Keeps it to himself unless lied to badly.
- **Suspicion on arrival:** stowaways +0; castaways +1; forged papers +1 (+3 if Marty catches a lie); Lunna's note +1; pirate theatre +2.

**Supper (ends Act I for every approach):** Marty, Rokko, Agatha, Yanni and the Cook in the mess. Rokko stops mid-sentence, looks at the ceiling: the storm is coming before sunrise. He talks Marty and the Captain into diving tonight (their own money, no second try). The Captain orders the ship readied. **Strike three Descent segments in front of the players.** Act II begins here, at beat 1.

## 8. Goal 1: The documents

**Wanted:** the charts (sonar position and depth profile), the notebook (decoded folklore correlations), and the folio that ties them together. Copy beats steal; stealing beats nothing. Stolen documents come back via Ikke in a few days.

**Copying:** 1 hour per part by hand; 1 minute per part with Marty's camera-glasses (he wears them by day; they're on his desk only when he sleeps). Lunna's copies are available only through her counter-offer or when she decides to leave.

| Document | Beat 1 | Beat 2 | Beats 3–6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Charts | Wet lab cupboard (Litta there) | Wet lab empty | Empty in beat 3; Agatha and Litta in beats 4–5; Agatha alone in beat 6 |
| Notebook | Agatha's satchel at supper | On the table during the interview | Her cabin desk, beside the sleep-log; cabin empty from beat 3 |
| Folio | Captain's safe, bridge | Same | Same |

Agatha names all three locations in the interview.

**The dictated pages.** Three notebook pages in Agatha's hand that she doesn't remember writing: SC dictation.

- Page 1: the anomaly is a natural shoal; call the dive off.
- Page 2: wrong depth and position figures.
- Page 3: an order to burn the notebook if the dive goes ahead, ending mid-sentence.
- They contradict her charts; the charts are true. A copier must flag them: Investigation DC 12 (pressure and ink) or Nature/History DC 12 (figures clash). An unflagged copy is Goal 1 partial.
- Copying the notebook gives Agatha's clue (Goal 3) and Lunna's proof lever.
- Read aloud inside a bubble: the waltz stutters; the reader gains SC Influence +1.

**Routes:**

- **Marty's glasses:** Persuasion DC 12 to "try them on" (at supper, or "to watch the cable go down"), or Sleight of Hand DC 16 to lift them. He notices within the hour. They record anything said near them.
- **Agatha herself:** Persuasion DC 10 and she explains everything and reads from her notebook. Takes most of the prep; Marty listens in. Only the Captain opens the safe.
- **Locks:** chart room DC 13 (night), Agatha's cabin DC 12, wet lab cupboard DC 10. The Cook passes once an hour with trays: Stealth DC 14. If he catches someone, he asks what they're *really* after.
- **Yanni:** fetches anything for a toy (Persuasion DC 8; DC 15 without one). Doesn't know what a document is for: brings the Captain's log first and talks about it at the winch.
- **Take everything and leave in the storm:** same locks; Athletics DC 12 to reach the boat with a satchel. Goal 1 partial; the Reckless notices at dawn.
- **Show Agatha the box working:** she trades a copy for the demonstration and the schematics (Persuasion DC 13; Marty 20+ ft away). She keeps the knowledge of magic.
- **Mind magic on Agatha** (sleep then read; suggestion; dream-walking her memory, Arcana DC 15): spell + surge, Tikk 20+ ft from Marty. Inside the bubble her SC influence is jammed: she may wake mid-spell, furious and unfiltered.
- **Beat 2 window:** the Cook teaches Yanni to sharpen a blade in the galley. The Cook's-rounds Stealth becomes routine.

### Folio: the Captain's safe (bridge, arcane lock)

Unpickable. Detect Magic inside a bubble shows the lock. The helmsman is always at the wheel.

**Windows:**

- **Beat 2, best:** Captain at the Alvin hatch; Marty in his study, so the box can run.
- **Beat 6, second:** Captain midship, Marty at the winch; the box can run.
- **Beats 3–4:** the Captain is on the bridge.
- **Beats 4–5:** Marty is at the crane controls directly below, within 20 ft: no box.
- **Beat 1:** quiet, but everyone is at supper.

**Ways in:**

- **Past the helmsman:** Stealth DC 12 while he watches the sea; Deception or Performance DC 10 to make him look away for a round; Yanni's tea. A caught PC: Suspicion +1.
- **The key** (Captain's coat pocket): Yanni borrows it for a toy (Persuasion DC 8; DC 15 without), returns it within the scene and tells everyone. Or distract the Captain (Persuasion or Performance DC 12) while another PC lifts it (Sleight of Hand DC 16; failure is a ledger mark, no alarm). Or a wax impression (thieves' tools DC 14). In beats 5–6 he's midship, where the Cook has advantage to spot theft.
- **Knock inside the bubble:** suppresses the lock for 10 minutes (Dispel Magic ends it). The helmsman sees it and keeps it; the Captain perceives magic anyway. Surge roll.
- **Take it below:** unbolt the safe (workshop tools, a beat), carry it down below the main deck (Athletics DC 12; the crew help, politely, and remember), cast Knock or Dispel Magic there. Leaked magic is enough: no surge, no witnesses. It just works.
- **Force:** hammering fails (DC 25+, rings like a gong). The decompression chamber in the launch bay: unbolt and carry the safe (Athletics DC 12), read the controls (Investigation DC 12), overpressure it. It bursts after a beat with a bang the whole ship hears; a poor reading soaks the pages (legible but smeared). Fallback: carry out the whole safe: Goal 1 partial, Suspicion +2 or more, ledger entry.

### Charts: the wet lab cupboard

DC 10, or Athletics DC 8 to pop it (noisy). About a dozen rolls inside. Picking the right ones is DC 15; each skill adds something:

- **Investigation:** the worn, annotated prints, and that Lunna already copied them.
- **History:** the stepped depth profile matches the drowned-city legends.
- **Nature:** which trace is too regular to be a shoal or reef.

On a miss: guess, or take everything. Taking everything means Agatha notices at beat 4 (Suspicion +2); a wrong guess means the Resistance gets junk (Goal 1 partial).

### Notebook

- **Beat 1:** in Agatha's satchel at supper. Lifting it: Sleight of Hand DC 16 (the Cook has advantage to spot it). Safer: Persuasion DC 10 to get her explaining while someone borrows Marty's glasses (Persuasion DC 12).
- **After the interview:** her cabin desk beside the sleep-log (door DC 12). Yanni fetches the sleep-log first, which is Lunna's proof.
- Agatha can't destroy the folio (it's in the safe). She can lose the notebook.

## 9. Goal 2: Lunna

**Success:** Lunna safe (off the ship, or staying under a deal), nobody hurt, Marty none the wiser. No cover story needed.

**She** doesn't want to go, refused once already, knows more than the PCs and knows they can't promise anything. Promises do nothing; she smiles at them.

**Levers (only these work):**

- **Proof she endangers Marty now:** the dictated pages, a PC glowing (surge 16), Agatha's blackout in front of her.
- **A way to say what she needs to Marty:** the private talk in beat 5.
- **The dragon:** in beat 6 she knows Rokko is lost; grief makes her movable.

**Hints.** In every conversation she says one true line inside a lie, each pointing at a lever: "I have to see it myself", "I can't say it to his face", and a stillness at Rokko's storm call. Insight DC 14 (or a contest vs her +9) shows which line is true. On a failure the hint comes at her second conversation.

### Determination clock (0–4, starts at 1)

Shown to players as "Lunna goes to Marty".

| Beat | Where she is | +1 (hardens) | −1 (softens) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 · Supper | Mess, with everyone | A PC lies and she catches it (passive Insight 16) | A PC gives her a true fact instead of a promise (e.g. the order to keep the device away from the grey cat) |
| 2 · Interview | Agatha's cabin | A PC is caught eavesdropping or lying (passive Perception 13) | Agatha fails her save in front of her, or the PCs supply the pages or the sleep-log |
| 3 | Alone in Marty's study | She finds Marty's note about the journalist who avoids him, or a PC rifles the desk in front of her | A PC shows her a safe way to reach him: beat 5, crane controls, nobody else around |
| 4 | Rokko's cabin (Marty alone at the crane controls) | She sees Rokko's sea drawings and goes to Marty now, unless delayed | A PC helps her decide what to say (a stranger's story), or delays her with an errand |
| 5 | Crane controls, alone with Marty | An interruption leaves her unfinished, or force is planned | The PCs keep Yanni, Rokko and the Captain's chair away |
| 6 | Life capsule, with the bosun; Rokko is lost | She heads for the rail with Marty | The PCs are at the capsule with a boat |

Props: Marty's note about the journalist; Rokko's notebook of sea drawings.

**Reading the clock at beat 5:** 0–1, she tells it as a stranger's story and Marty keeps it. 2, half the truth: Marty learns she knows him but not who she is (Suspicion +2). 3–4, she says the name: Goal 2 blown. **At beat 6:** 2 or below, she leaves willingly if a boat is there; above that she goes to the rail with Marty.

### Ways out

- **Staged pirate kidnapping** in the storm: only with her consent, and not "taken twice by the same pirates" unless a lever has landed. Performance DC 12 by the boarders; her Deception DC 10 to struggle. Kot Kong must be at the winch or he intervenes.
- **She "falls" overboard** and the rowboat takes her out of sight: Athletics DC 12 for a safe drop; she fails the water-fear save on purpose. Yanni sees her jump if on deck (Perception 15).
- **Her counter-offer:** her copies of the charts and half the notebook, plus a story: *tell Purrow the Glowing Cats have me.* She stays. Insight DC 14 to see she's bluffing about what she'd do if refused. Goal 1 partial for free.
- **The private talk (beat 5):** alone with Marty at the crane controls; the outcome depends on the clock. Keep Rokko away. No box: Marty is off-limits.
- **The dragon (beat 6):** she goes if the PCs are at the life capsule with a boat. If they miss the boat, so does she.
- **The letter channel:** she stays; a dead-drop through Ikke, letters read by the Resistance first. Persuasion DC 12; write the first coded letter at the table. Goal 2 partial.

### The interview (beat 2, Agatha's cabin)

Mostly Agatha's search for Catlantis. Listening in: Stealth DC 13 at the door, Perception DC 12 at a porthole, or carrying trays under the Cook's bargain. Listeners learn where the three documents are and what Lunna is after (who has read the pages, how Agatha sleeps).

Lunna asks Agatha to read one dictated page aloud: **Agatha's Cha save DC 13 at +4** (cabin deck).

- **Pass:** informational scene; no lever moves.
- **Fail:** Agatha loses her place mid-sentence; the words aren't hers. The proof lever lands (−1), Agatha's Influence +1, listeners get Agatha's clue (Goal 3).
- **Natural 1–5:** as a fail, and Agatha goes for her notebook to burn it (page 3). Improvise; if it's lost, only Lunna's half copy survives. This happens only in this scene.

### Blown outright

Marty hears the name Urrch; a PC casts on Marty in a bubble; or the Cook hands Marty the coded letters and the spare box together ("they looked like his handwriting"). The PCs get one scene to stop it (Persuasion DC 14). Interruptions that work: Yanni with tea, the Captain's chair, Purrow's lantern hail, Kot Kong's winch trouble. If it goes through, SC pressure on Marty rises and the family reunion happens early, in the worst way, just before Rokko is lost.

### Results

- **Full:** proof landed, the beat 5 talk goes as a stranger's story, she leaves in the boat in beat 6. She hands over her copies (Goal 1 partial for free), rejoins the Resistance later and trusts the team.
- **Partial:** she stays by her own choice under a deal or the letter channel; the PCs decide whether to carry the lie "the Glowing Cats have me". Variant: she leaves but Marty half-knows (Suspicion +2).
- **Fail:** she says the name, the Cook hands Marty the letters and box, or anyone is hurt.

## 11. Goal 3 (bonus): Recruits

Repeatable: every gifted cat recruited counts. Full: recruited. Partial: an agreement to stay in touch, or taken by force (only if compromised or the documents are lost).

### Agatha

The Glowing Cats steer her toward wrong data, calling off the dive and destroying her research.

**Triggers:** rain begins; she reads her dictated pages; she is within 10 ft of open water at night; Rokko reaches the city; surge 4 or 16 near her. On each: **Cha save DC 13.** Fail: Influence +1 (starts at 2; the rail at 5). Natural 1–5 matters only in the beat 2 interview.

- **Saves below decks:** +2 on the cabin deck, +4 on the lower deck. A full beat on the lower deck lowers Influence by 1, once.
- **A full minute in a running bubble** (Marty 20+ ft away): Influence −1. Persuasion DC 12 per minute to keep her there. She notices the change and wants to know why; recruitment opens.
- **Litta's remedy** (earth magic and herbs, cast in the bubble): Medicine DC 12 to get her to drink. Influence −1. Litta becomes a contact (opens her recruitment).
- **The contradiction:** get her to lay the pages beside her charts (Persuasion DC 10) and she believes it. **Before the dive:** Descent +1, but the storm comes faster: W1 and W2 happen together in the first window segment, the cable comes up cut in the second, the third is calm before dawn. The window is still three segments. **After the dive:** no clock change.
- **Holding the rail (W3, with Kot Kong):** Athletics contest vs her +5; four successes before three failures. Inside a bubble she fights back unfiltered and remembers.
- **Recruiting her** once something real has been shown: Persuasion DC 14, or DC 10 if she has seen the box work or the contradiction. She comes willingly, or says "not yet" and keeps the secret.
- **Taking her by force:** only if compromised or the documents are lost. Lifeboat in the storm; Sleight of Hand DC 14, Athletics DC 12. The Captain acts.
- **Confronting the program** (mind or dream magic in the bubble, speaking to what speaks through her): Arcana DC 15, surge, then Wis save DC 14 or SC Influence 1 on the PC. The Glowing Cats now know that PC's face. The voice offers a trade: Marty for Agatha.

**Clue trail (any two start it):** the dictated pages; her sleep-log; Rokko: "She goes quiet when it rains — like she's listening"; Yanni saw her "talking to the water" two nights ago; Litta's plants thrive on the cabin deck but wilt where Agatha sleeps.

### Litta

- **First time inside a bubble:** she casts with control.
- **Recruit:** Persuasion DC 12 after she has cast inside the bubble. She asks to keep the box: Tikk refuses (as a PC, his player's call); Purrow has a spare.
- **Refusal:** she has to see what happens to Marty and Rokko first.
- **Kidnap:** only if she refuses and the players choose it. She fights back inside a bubble, and Kot Kong loves her.
- Either way she ends the session knowing the Resistance exists.
- **Marty's talk with Litta (beat 2, his study, door shut):** she talks about the magic unprompted. Stealth DC 13 at the door, Perception DC 12 through a porthole or wall. Rokko nearby senses a listener.
- **The hold (clue):** she feeds the stowaway cat and coaxes a sprout without a spell. Keeping her secret earns trust; telling the Captain loses her.

## 13. Conclusion

### The knot

The vision can only be tied to reality at the knot: Rokko going down. The mages start tying when the Descent clock fills; tying takes the three window segments. Every delay moves the knot with the dive.

**The mages, after the reveal (read aloud):** "We see what will happen, not when. The boy goes down — that always happens. That's the knot. The moment he does, we start tying your night to the real one, and it takes us until the storm is done. Whatever you've made by the end of it — that's what we tie. If it isn't enough, we let go and you wake up."

### The Commit (end of W3, every run)

- **Check:** Goals 1 and 2 at least partly fulfilled, and no clock full. Goal 3 is a bonus.
- **Before the check** it's still a rehearsal: anything that ends the run resets as usual, even with every goal done.
- **Pass:** for one bar the waltz plays in a key nobody has heard, from everywhere, through the storm. Far away, two cats breathe out. Then the Pull.
- **Fail:** the dream ends; loop.
- **Party already on the Floppy:** the mages keep them out of the dreamchamber: "Not yet. Hold it." Their sleeping bodies are visible through the door.

### The Crossing

200 ft of sea between the Reckless and the Floppy. Three legs. Every PC rolls once per leg; the leg succeeds if half or more succeed. A failed leg costs one Toll and the crossing goes on. Before W1 the sea is calm: roll only leg 1.

| Leg | Approaches |
| --- | --- |
| 1 · Over the side | Lifeboat: Athletics or thieves' tools DC 12 (the davits squeal; the crew come to help, politely). Their own rowboat, if a sailor hasn't hauled it aboard. Jump: Athletics DC 13 plus the water-fear save (none in a bubble). Across to Purrow alongside in W2: Acrobatics DC 12 |
| 2 · The gap | Row: Athletics DC 13. Steer: Survival or water vehicles DC 13. Bail: Con save DC 12. Keep cargo dry: Sleight of Hand DC 12. A helpful earth or water spell succeeds outright, then the surge roll; cranking in the boat is Str DC 10 or the box slides |
| 3 · Up the side | Purrow's nets: Athletics DC 10. Anything passed up on a successful leg is safe |

**Tolls** (the table picks one per failed leg; no repeats):

- **Overboard:** one PC goes in. Athletics DC 13 to grab the line, or in the water until the next leg.
- **Lost cargo:** one held item goes into the sea. It stays in the present.
- **Battered:** 2d6 cold or bludgeoning to everyone.
- **Slow:** the leg takes the rest of the segment; the clock ticks.
- **Questioned:** a helpful crew cat asks an honest question ("What's in the satchel?"). Truth: no tick, but the cat keeps the item "for safekeeping". Lie: Deception at disadvantage; fail, Suspicion +1. Push past: Suspicion +1; the cat remembers.

**Three failed legs:** the boat capsizes. Everyone in the water; Purrow throws lines; Lunna first.

### Static (W3, every run)

The waking dragon thinks it's still mid-battle; its reflexes scramble the Filter. Roll d6 at the start of each Crossing leg and each round of a fight; otherwise once per scene.

| d6 | Static | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Dead air** | The Filter drops out until the next roll: no water fear, no graciousness; cats react like real people and remember. Magic works anywhere, no box, no surge |
| 2 | **Undertow** | Water fear becomes longing: each cat within 30 ft of open water, Cha save DC 12 or moves its speed toward it (none in a bubble). Kot Kong and the Captain fish sailors out. Marty saves at disadvantage. PCs have advantage on the jump |
| 3 | **Howl** | The bubble grows to 40 ft; every spell in it surges automatically. If Marty is now inside, Tikk stops cranking |
| 4 | **Too kind** | Disadvantage on every check involving another cat, including helping. The crew help only "after you" |
| 5 | **Glow** | Cats with SC Influence glow. Each such PC: Wis save DC 13 or Influence +1. Agatha makes her Cha save |
| 6 | **The Eye** | The sea opens somewhere in the dark and something looks up. Cha save DC 15 or frightened until the next roll. Anyone who meets its gaze is **Seen by the dragon** |

### Draconic speakers

- **Box off:** storm and wind. Once per segment: Insight or Arcana DC 15 to notice the gusts have grammar.
- **Box on:** speakers inside a running bubble hear very old Draconic. Everyone else hears weather. Keep Marty 20+ ft away. If Tikk is a speaker, the other must stay within 20 ft of him.

### The Pull (committed run only)

The dragon wakes believing it is still in its last battle, under the city the Glowing Cats dropped on it. The flashback catches the dreamers mid-tie and throws them into Catlantis, about a month before the fall.

- Only the dreamers go: the PCs and Tikk, with the box. The Floppy, Purrow and the mages stay.
- They keep what they hold, their HP, and their marks (SC Influence, Seen by the dragon).
- Everything else follows the cargo rule.

## 14. Endings and epilogue

Read the goal table at the Commit. On a committed run this is what the present keeps.

- **Blown** (Marty learns who she is): GC pressure on Marty rises; the family reunion happens early, in the worst way.
- **Clean job** (Lunna out, documents copied, Agatha's hold broken and/or Litta recruited): the Resistance has the map and a gifted druid; Lunna rejoins later; Agatha is clear-headed.
- **Loud job** (documents stolen): they come back via Ikke in a few days; the Reckless knows it was robbed; the ledger has entries.
- **Half job** (Lunna stays): a controlled Lunna aboard; the letter channel becomes a campaign device.
- **Dark job** (Agatha destroyed the documents; taken by force): only Lunna's copies survive; Agatha is a Resistance prisoner-guest.
- **Owned** (Agatha over the rail): she survives; she is the Glowing Cats' voice in the campaign.
- **Still aboard at the end of W3:** committed, the Pull takes them from the deck and the Captain's log gets the strangest entry of his career. Uncommitted, the dream ends.

**The present also keeps:** four empty cots on the Floppy, still warm, wet with seawater that tastes wrong; whatever was handed to a non-dreamer; the Reckless remembering the castaways and every lie it caught. If Lunna was in the boat with them, she is alone in it at dawn, holding oars four cats were pulling a second ago.

**Epilogue cards** (one line each, fill at the table):

- **Marty:** what he wrote in his notebook the night he lost Rokko.
- **Agatha:** what she thinks the blank hour was.
- **Captain:** the log entry, and the ledger entry.
- **The Cook:** what he served the next morning, and to whom.
- **Yanni:** what he tells everyone.
- **Litta:** whether her plants grew back.
- **Kot Kong:** who he decided to like.
- **Lunna:** the first line of her first letter to Ikke.
- **Purrow:** what he tells the cell about the empty cots.
- **The mages:** what took the thread out of their hands.
