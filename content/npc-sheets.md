# Operation: Wet Pages — NPC Character Sheets & Voice Notes

2026-09-19 · Companion to *Operation Wet Pages — DM Document* · D&D 2024 (5.5e)

## How to use this file

**What this is.** Nine named NPCs from the Reckless built as full 2024 characters at their own levels, each with a sheet, a habits-and-voice section, and table notes. Not covered: Tikk, Purrow and the unnamed crew.

**Level ladder** (chosen from your ranges; swap any of them and the math below still works, since every number is derived from level, class and scores):

| Level | NPC | Proficiency bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | Yanni | +2 |
| 4 | Litta, Kot Kong | +2 |
| 6 | Rokko, Agatha | +3 |
| 7 | Marty | +3 |
| 8 | Lunna | +3 |
| 9 | The Cook | +4 |
| 11 | Captain Sevastian | +4 |

**How they are built.** Each sheet follows the PC rules: hit die maximum at 1st level then fixed averages, saving throws from the first class only, feats and ability score improvements taken at the levels the classes grant them, and spell slots from the multiclass table. Species chassis are borrowed from the 2024 species list to give each cat a legal frame (Human for most, Halfling for Yanni, Wood Elf for Litta, Goliath for Kot Kong), and every cat then gets the DM Document's cat package. Class features are summarised by name and effect; check the 2024 PHB for exact wording before you rely on an edge case.

**Cat package** (from the DM Document's species rule, applied to every cat, not to the Captain). Darkvision 60 ft., +1 Dexterity, and the Speedy feat free (Speed +10 ft., Dash ignores difficult terrain, Opportunity Attacks against you have Disadvantage; its own +1 Dex or Con is included in the scores). Speeds below already include the +10 ft.

**Claws.** Every cat has natural claws: unarmed strikes with Finesse, dealing 1d4 + Dex Slashing damage.

**Filter (outside a music-box bubble).** Disadvantage on attack rolls, damaging spells, Intimidation, and any check meant to hurt or coerce another cat (never against fish). Cats answer violence and theft with comic politeness ("Oh, do you need that? Take it, take it") but remember. They cannot perceive or retain magic. Within 5 ft of open water a cat makes a DC 12 Charisma save or spends the turn moving away; sailors of the Reckless have Advantage. Inside a running bubble none of this applies.

**What counts as magic.** Spellcasting, Bardic Inspiration, Innate Sorcery, Wild Shape, Channel Divinity, elixirs, infusions, Flash of Genius and every magic item work only inside a bubble, and every spell cast there risks a wild surge. Martial features (Rage, Sneak Attack, Second Wind, maneuvers) work everywhere. Every sheet lists its magic under a "Bubble only" heading.

**These NPCs outclass the PCs on purpose.** They are level 2 to 11 against a level-4 party. What keeps it fair is the Filter (cats do not fight cats), the rule that no NPC dies, and the clocks. Only Captain Sevastian is not Filtered, and he acts only when "a reason" appears; if the party gives him one, the DM Document already accepts a TPK. Keep the crew's fight against the fish to one line of narration per round so it does not eat the PCs' fight.

**Numbers from the DM Document that are kept exactly.** Marty: Insight +6 (passive 16, so the Deception DC 16) and Athletics +1 (the W3 rail contest). Agatha: Athletics +5, Cha 14 and Wis 14, save proficiencies Str/Con only, Cha save +2 against her DC 13 triggers. The Cook: Perception +7. The Captain: passive Insight 14 (the forged-papers DC 14) and passive Perception 14.

**Numbers that move with level, and what to do.** Lunna's Deception is +9, so use a contested roll if you want a fair fight (your DC 14 for spotting her bluff is fine as a table-set DC). The Cook's Stealth +13 and passive Insight 21 make him very hard to beat by roll: keep your fixed DC 14 for the P1 knife-lesson window and treat passive scores as the truth the rest of the time.

**Where the voices come from.** Marty, Rokko, Litta and Yanni come from Book 1 (chapters 1–5) plus *Основные герои*; the Cook, Captain Sevastian, Kot Kong, Agatha and Lunna do not speak on the page yet, so their voices are extrapolated from *Основные герои* and the DM Document and are first drafts. A dagger (†) marks a mechanic or habit invented here; strike any you dislike. Russian tics from the book appear where they help.

## 1 · Marty (Мартин «Марти» Урч)

*Level 7 · Artificer (Alchemist) 7 · Human chassis (Small) with cat package · Sage background · Neutral Good*

**AC** 14 (studded leather and Dex; his infusions are inert outside a bubble) **Initiative** +2 (12)
**HP** 38 (8 at 1st level, then 5 per level, Con +0)
**Speed** 40 ft. **Proficiency Bonus** +3

| Ability | Score | Mod | Save | Ability | Score | Mod | Save |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Str | 12 | +1 | +1 | Int | 17 | +3 | +6 |
| Dex | 15 | +2 | +2 | Wis | 16 | +3 | +3 |
| Con | 10 | +0 | +3 | Cha | 8 | −1 | −1 |

**Skills** Arcana +6, History +6, Insight +6, Investigation +6, Perception +6
**Tools** Tinker's Tools and Cook's Utensils (Expertise), Thieves' Tools, Alchemist's Supplies, Herbalism Kit, Calligrapher's Supplies, three musical instruments
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 16, Passive Insight 16
**Languages** Common, a dead scholarly tongue (reads it)
**Feats and ASIs** Magic Initiate (Wizard) from Sage; Musician (Human Versatile); Skillful (Human: Insight); Speedy (cat); Chef at Artificer 4 (+1 Wis)

**Class features (Artificer 7).** Tinker's Magic, Replicate Magic Item (infusions inert outside a bubble), Right Tool for the Job, Tool Expertise. Alchemist: Experimental Elixir, Alchemical Savant, Alchemist Spells.
**Feat features.** *Chef:* on a short rest he cooks for up to 7 creatures, each regaining an extra 1d8 HP from Hit Dice; three Bolstering Treats per long rest, each granting Temp HP equal to PB (3). *Musician:* a song after a rest gives Heroic Inspiration to up to 3 allies; he never admits he can sing.

**Attacks.** *Claws:* +5, reach 5 ft, 4 (1d4 + 2) Slashing damage.

**Bubble only: spellcasting.** Int, spell save DC 14, +6 to hit. Slots: four 1st, three 2nd. Cantrips: Guidance, Mending, Message, Mage Hand. Prepared: Cure Wounds, Healing Word, Detect Magic, Sanctuary, Aid, Lesser Restoration, Melf's Acid Arrow, plus Flaming Sphere reflavoured as a boiling-kettle sphere dealing Bludgeoning damage (no fire in this world). Flash of Genius (reaction, add +3 to a check or save within 30 ft, 3/long rest). He has never cast knowingly, and the box never runs within 20 ft of him; if it does anyway, treat the run as blown because he perceives magic and keeps it.

**Story traits**

***Filtered Cat.*** Cat template. Advantage on the water-fear save (he has rowed a boat across a sea). In W3 the Filter does not stop him at all: grief overrides it and he walks to the rail.
***Inviolable.*** Marty and Rokko must not be harmed by anyone. If either dies, the crew is retracted and the run restarts (DM Document, Inviolable rules). Stopping him at the W3 rail: Athletics contest against his +1, Persuasion DC 15, or simply holding on.
***Listener.*** After a minute of conversation he can state the other cat's position accurately; the DM answers one question about what the speaker actually believes, no roll. Deceiving him: DC 16.
***Analyst's Blind Spot.*** Disadvantage on Insight checks to read a creature's feelings or mood. He hears the sense of what is said and can miss a change in behaviour entirely. Lies are another matter.
***Camera-Glasses.*** Magnifying lenses (he reads a page across a room) with a built-in camera, film for six hours of video. Photographing a page takes 1 minute per part. Anything said within 30 ft while he wears them is on film. If the glasses are lifted he notices within the hour.
***Psychoanalysis Session.*** One hour, private (Location 9, door shut), by appointment. Needs no box: one completed session lowers Agatha's Influence by 1. Route D: Lunna tells "someone else's story" as a client (her Deception DC 13 against his Insight); he hears it and keeps it.
***Mint Tea †.*** Once per scene, as a Bonus Action, he presses a hot mug on someone; the drinker has Advantage on its next water-fear save this scene.
***Claws Out †.*** Reaction, once per scene: when a friend within 5 ft is about to be badly hurt, he makes one Claws attack against the aggressor. Aghast at himself, he can take no Reactions and has Disadvantage on Insight checks until the end of his next turn.

**Gear.** Camera-glasses, knitted scarf, notebook and pencil, penknife, waterproof cloak-tent, studded leather under a mackintosh.

### Voice and habits

**Register and pace.** Precise, gently academic, never quite at ease. Whole tidy sentences and formal courtesy to everyone (in Russian he says «вы» even to Rokko). Slow: he leaves gaps to check what he just said, listens about twice as long as he talks, and dodges being questioned by asking first (Book ch. 2: to avoid questions, ask them).

**Verbal tics.** «Вот же ж!» when flustered; in English, "Well, isn't that just—!". "Let me make sure I understand you." "That is a hypothesis, not a conclusion." He addresses himself in the second person by his full name: "Martin, pull yourself together. There is no tea here and no fireplace. Finish what you started. Tea comes after."

**Habits and business.** Twists the frame to change the glasses' magnification. Sharpens a pencil with a penknife while listening. Writes one-line notebook entries ("I am happy."). Chews each mouthful thirty-two times, or twenty-three at a push. Computes rations aloud. Mint tea, warm milk (Rokko teases him for it), a fireplace when overwhelmed. When shaken he sorts belongings into neat rows, papers to papers, pebbles to pebbles. His movements are nervous but unhurried. He sings well and hides it. He cooks superbly and makes any cabin cosy within a day.

**Sample lines.** Meeting a stranger: "Forgive the question, but why the anchor chain and not the gangway?" Opening a session: "Sit wherever you like. You needn't say anything. I find people usually do." Catching a lie: "No — forgive me, that doesn't fit with what you said earlier. Take your time." Under the Filter, when robbed: "Oh, were you holding those? By all means. (beat) I'm going to write down that this happened."

**Under pressure.** The colder and more formal he gets, the more it hurts. Losing Rokko (W3): silence, then extreme politeness, then he moves.

**At the table.** He catches facts, not feelings: a frightened PC telling the truth passes, a cheerful liar does not. Hearing the name "Adams" blows Lunna's cover. Never in a bubble.

## 2 · Rokko (Рокко Россо)

*Level 6 · Sorcerer (Aberrant Mind) 6 · Human chassis (Medium) with cat package · Entertainer background · Chaotic Good*

**AC** 13 (unarmoured, Dex) **Initiative** +6 (16; Alert adds PB)
**HP** 38 (d6 at 1st level, then 4 per level, Con +2)
**Speed** 40 ft. **Proficiency Bonus** +3

| Ability | Score | Mod | Save | Ability | Score | Mod | Save |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Str | 8 | −1 | −1 | Int | 10 | +0 | +0 |
| Dex | 16 | +3 | +3 | Wis | 12 | +1 | +1 |
| Con | 14 | +2 | +5 | Cha | 19 | +4 | +7 |

**Skills** Acrobatics +6, Deception +7, Insight +4, Performance +7, Persuasion +7
**Tools** Three musical instruments
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 11, Passive Insight 14
**Languages** Common; a little bad French when hungry
**Feats and ASIs** Musician (Entertainer); Alert (Human Versatile); Skillful (Human: Persuasion); Speedy (cat); Cha +2 at Sorcerer 4

**Class features (Sorcerer 6).** Innate Sorcery, Font of Magic (6 Sorcery Points), Metamagic (Subtle Spell, Twinned Spell), Sorcerous Restoration. Aberrant Mind: Psionic Spells, Telepathic Speech, Psionic Sorcery, Psychic Defenses (Resistance to Psychic damage, Advantage on saves against Charmed and Frightened). *Alert:* Initiative Swap. *Musician:* a song after a rest gives Heroic Inspiration to up to 3 allies.

**Attacks.** *Claws:* +6, reach 5 ft, 5 (1d4 + 3) Slashing damage.

**Bubble only: spellcasting.** Cha, spell save DC 15, +7 to hit. Slots: four 1st, three 2nd, three 3rd. Cantrips: Mind Sliver, Message, Minor Illusion, Prestidigitation, Dancing Lights. Always prepared (Aberrant Mind): Arms of Hadar, Calm Emotions, Detect Thoughts, Dissonant Whispers, Hunger of Hadar, Sending. Also prepared: Sleep, Suggestion, Hypnotic Pattern, Charm Person. He has never cast knowingly and must never be near a bubble.

**Story traits**

***Filtered Cat.*** Cat template. Advantage on the water-fear save; he has always fantasised about the sea.
***Inviolable.*** As Marty.
***Mood Reader.*** No roll: he knows the emotional state of any cat within 30 ft (nervous, excited, grieving) but never the reason unless they say it. He keeps what he learns to himself unless lied to badly.
***Many Ears.*** He follows up to three conversations at different distances at once and can answer any of them.
***Forecast (never explain at the table).*** His whiskers and nose call weather days ahead: rain timing, wind direction, speed. It is really dream magic and the dragon's approach, the one kind of magic that skips the bubble. He calls the storm at supper.
***Notices the Bubble.*** If Rokko ever stands inside a running music-box bubble, he stops, looks around, and says something like "You guys aren't really here, are you?" Everyone inside with him vanishes from the outside's point of view. Treat it as a run-ender and message the affected players privately (DM Document, The frame).
***Lamplight Glow.*** In lamplight his red fur catches gold like scales (Book ch. 2). Harmless, and unnoticed by him; unsettling to observant PCs.
***Fixed Point.*** He calls the descent early at the end of Act I, goes down in the Alvin in P3, and does not come back on any attempt. Nothing the PCs do changes that.
***Encouragement †.*** Non-magical, so it works anywhere. Bonus Action, once per creature per scene: he praises a cat and its next ability check has Advantage. He prefers the unsure and the underrated.

**Gear.** Raincoat, high boots, older brother's suit, suspenders that hold nothing up, a rucksack of herring, duck crisps and fizzy drinks.

### Voice and habits

**Register and pace.** Fast, warm, cheeky, run-on. He jumps topics, talks over himself, and answers three people at once. Book ch. 2: he chats, listens, doodles and drums his claws on the desk at the same time. Nicknames flow: «братан» ("bro"), «хвост» ("tail", slang). With Marty he plays exaggerated formality: "Professor Urch", "Doctor Marty", «вы».

**Verbal tics.** Mid-sentence he stops, looks at the ceiling and states the weather. "Wanna bet?" and then, in the middle of a heated argument, he grins and says, "Actually, you were right all along." When tired or hungry he reaches for French: «Же не манж па ду жур».

**Habits and business.** Eats everything at once: smoked herring, duck crisps, fizzy drinks, carrot buns. Sits still for about ten minutes. Passes paper notes ("Great day!", "Out through the window!"). Takes notes as single words, syllables and pictures, and can rebuild a whole lecture from them. Sniffs the air; his whiskers hum before storms. Bored by cats who bring nothing new; adores oddballs, the widely read, and unsure talented cats to whom he hands out compliments like coins.

**Storytelling.** He casts himself as the brave hero and Marty as a harmless eccentric (Book ch. 5). When stung he gets even by telling the room something embarrassing about Marty (ch. 4).

**Sample lines.** Welcome: "Sit, sit. You look like you've been rowing since dinner. Have a herring." Reading a nervous PC: "You're not shipwrecked, you're *excited*." Under the Filter, robbed: "Oh, take it, take it! Actually, no, keep it — you needed it more than I did." Calling the storm: "Storm's coming before sunrise. Not the kind you'll like."

**Under pressure.** The jokes speed up. In W2 and W3 they stop; he goes quiet and unusually gentle.

**At the table.** A "bad lie" is one that insults his read of you. He never explains the Forecast.

**Tools** Calligrapher's Supplies
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft.; Passive Perception 12

**Class features.** *Fighter 2:* Fighting Style (Unarmed Fighting), Second Wind (2/day, 1d10 + 2), Weapon Mastery, Action Surge (1/day), Tactical Mind. *Cleric 4:* Divine Order (Thaumaturge), Channel Divinity (2 uses), Life Domain (Disciple of Life, Preserve Life). *Grappler:* Punch and Grab, Fast Wrestler, attacks against a creature she has grappled have Advantage.

**Attacks.** *Unarmed Strike:* +5, reach 5 ft, 5 (1d6 + 2) Bludgeoning damage; a creature she is grappling takes 1d4 Bludgeoning at the start of her turn. *Grapple / rail contest:* Athletics +5 (the DM Document's A4 rail contest).

**Bubble only: spellcasting.** Wis, spell save DC 13, +5 to hit. Slots: four 1st, three 2nd. Cantrips: Guidance, Light, Spare the Dying, Toll the Dead. Magic Initiate (Wizard): Mage Hand, Ray of Frost, and Feather Fall (1/long rest). She does not know any of this is magic. Inside a bubble her Influence is jammed and she may wake mid-spell, furious and un-Filtered.

**Story traits**

***Filtered Cat.*** Cat template. Advantage on the water-fear save (an aquatic scientist).
***Influence (Glowing Cats).*** The track starts at 2 and the rail is 5. Triggers: rain begins; she reads her dictated pages; she is within 10 ft of open water at night; Rokko reaches the city; wild surge 4 or 16 near her. Each trigger, she rolls a Charisma save against DC 13 with a +2 bonus (no proficiency). Fail: Influence +1. Natural 1–5: she tries to destroy the documents this scene (folio over the rail, notebook into the galley stove). At 5 she walks to the rail. Reduce Influence by 1 for a full minute inside a bubble, Litta's remedy, or a completed session with Marty.
***Blackouts.*** She loses an hour and does not know. Her sleep-log and three notebook pages are in her own handwriting, dictated by the Glowing Cats.
***Lecturer.*** Persuasion DC 10 to get her to explain her work (she loves this). Investigation DC 12 to get her to fetch the folio "to show you".

**Gear.** Sonar printouts, sleep-log, keys to the chart room and to her chest, the master folio (locked chest), the working notebook.

### Voice and habits

**Register and pace.** Confident, brisk, warm until you interrupt. A coach and lecturer in one: her father trained the city's carp-spinning team and demanded as much from her as from his athletes, and she thanks him for it. Headline claim first, evidence second. She explains her work to anyone who asks well, and every well-asked question earns a full lecture with folklore digressions.

**Verbal tics.** "Here's the thing." "Again — no, cleaner. You're rushing." "I'll finish, thank you" to anyone who cuts her off (she holds a chair in an all-male department). Sea proverbs: "Every port has a story about the sea taking back what it gave."

**Habits and business.** Taps sonar printouts. Paces the chart room. Flips the long locks of cream fur off her forehead when impatient. A hand on your shoulder to make a point. Quietly goes still when it rains, as if listening; Yanni saw her "talking to the water"; Litta's plants wilt where she sleeps. She does not remember the sleep-log.

**Influence ladder (voice cue).** Influence 2: herself. Influence 3: pauses mid-sentence and turns toward the water. Influence 4: "we" slips into her sentences. Influence 5: quiet, calm, "It's better this way," and the rail.

**Sample lines.** "You want to know what's down there? Sit. Ask properly and I'll draw you the whole city." "Nobody hands you a grant. You take the boat, you start the locator yourself." Under the Filter, when robbed: "Oh, the folio? Of course, of course — (frowns) I feel I ought to mind that."

**Under pressure.** Doubted, she gets sharper. Crowded, she gets physical. In a bubble she wakes furious: "You took my notebook. You *took* it."

**At the table.** Trades the science, not the pages: a demonstration of the box for a copy (Route F), with the campaign consequences flagged in the DM Document.

**Skills** Acrobatics +5, Athletics +8, History +4, Insight +4, Perception +4, Survival +8 (Expertise)

**Senses** Blind Fighting (blindsight 10 ft.); Passive Perception 14, Passive Insight 14
**Languages** Common, sailors' pidgin, the old sea-cant
**Feats and ASIs** Tavern Brawler (Sailor); Tough (Human Versatile); Blind Fighting (Ranger style); Defense (Fighter style); Str +2 at Ranger 4; Con +2 at Fighter 4

**Class features.** *Ranger 6:* Favored Enemy, Deft Explorer, Hunter's Lore, Hunter's Prey (Colossus Slayer: +1d8 once per turn against a creature already damaged), Extra Attack, Roving. *Fighter 5:* Second Wind (3/day, 1d10 + 5), Action Surge (1/day), Tactical Mind, Tactical Shift, Weapon Mastery, Student of War. *Battle Master:* four d8 superiority dice, maneuver save DC 16; Commander's Strike, Rally, Riposte.

**Attacks.** Extra Attack (two attacks). *Harpoon (Spear, Sap):* +8, reach 5 ft or thrown 20/60 ft., 8 (1d8 + 4) Piercing two-handed, 7 (1d6 + 4) one-handed; Colossus Slayer adds 4 (1d8).

**Dormant spellcasting.** Wis, spell save DC 12, +4 to hit; slots four 1st, two 2nd. He has never tried it and does not know there is anything to cast. The Filter's magic-blindness does not apply to him; ignorance is his only barrier.

**Story traits**

***Not a Cat.*** The Filter does nothing to him: no aggression penalty, no politeness, no fear of water, and he sees and remembers magic that other cats rationalise away. Attacks against him are not Filtered either.
***Sea-Born.*** Fished out of open water twenty years ago with a school of mackerel and never afraid of water since. A wooden leg replaces one below the knee, a roughly hewn stick.
***Unshockable.*** Immune to Frightened; cannot be Surprised while he sits in his chair.
***The Chair.*** Roll a d6 at the start of each scene: on a 1 he is already there, armchair and pipe, in the middle of the conversation. Nobody saw him arrive.
***The Ledger.*** He keeps a private ledger of what the PCs did, kept in the pouch on his belly. On a failed Deception (DC 12 for the castaways, DC 14 for forged papers) he sees through it and says nothing. A crew member hurt or the ship endangered is "a reason": then he acts calmly and completely, ordering the crew rather than brawling.

**Gear.** Harpoon, breastplate under a sea-coat, briar pipe, ledger, an armchair that is never where you left it.

### Voice and habits

**Register and pace.** Short sentences, long silences, dry as ship's biscuit. Unhurried, never raises his voice. Asks one question at a time and lets you fill the silence. Speaks as though everything has already happened once.

**Verbal tics.** "Mm." (he never says "I know"). "Tea?" "Say that again, the true way round." Ends a decision by tapping the peg twice on the deck.

**Habits and business.** Pipe smoke rings while he thinks; lets the pipe go out when he has decided. Appears, sits, listens. Writes in the ledger without looking at it. Hauls castaways out of the sea without asking why they were in it.

**Sample lines.** Meeting castaways: "You've come a long way in a small boat. Sit." At the rail: "Not here, Marty." Given a reason: "I said tea. I did not say you could leave." Asked about his pouch (†): "Everyone's got one. Haven't they?"

**Under pressure.** He gets quieter and slower, and the crew moves.

**At the table.** In a bubble the waltz makes him glance up and relight his pipe: "Nice tune." He is the one cat who can watch the party do magic and remember it.
