# Takedown Kit — Nonlethal Quick Incapacitation

Oct 13, 2026 · @Tagir

## Core rules

These are homebrew takedowns for 2024 rules (5.5e) and level 3 cats. Every takedown works only on an **unaware** target, and every one has a **noise** cost.

**Takedown DC** = 8 + proficiency bonus + Str or Dex modifier. That's about 13–14 at level 3.

**Unaware target.** A target is unaware if you're hidden from it (you have the Invisible condition from Hide), it's surprised and hasn't taken a turn yet, or it's distracted (see Lure). Against an aware target, takedowns either don't work or the target saves with advantage, as noted on each one.

| Noise | Who notices |
| --- | --- |
| Silent | Nobody, unless they can see it happen |
| Muffled | Anyone within 30 ft with passive Perception 13+ comes over to check. That's not an alarm yet |
| Loud | Everyone within 60 ft is alert and the alarm is raised |

**Waking up.** This replaces the normal 2024 knockout timing so the heist has a clock: a knocked-out guard wakes after 1d4 × 10 minutes and then goes looking.

## The takedowns

| Takedown | Cost | Noise | Aware target |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bonk | Action, melee | Muffled | Saves with advantage |
| Scruff | Action, free hand, cats only | Silent | Doesn't work |
| Pounce | Rider on Bonk or Scruff | Loud if the target saves | Doesn't work |
| Valerian drops | Action (Bonus Action with Fast Hands), 3 doses | Muffled | Works if it drinks or is held |
| Lure | Bonus Action | Silent | Works: that's the point |
| Splash | Action, range 15 ft, water | Loud | Works |
| Truss | Action (Bonus Action with Fast Hands), rope and gag | Silent | Target must be out or held |

### Bonk

Make a melee attack. On a hit, deal no damage. The target makes a Con save against your Takedown DC.

- **Fail:** Unconscious.
- **Success:** Winded. It can only whisper and can't take reactions until the end of its next turn, so you get one more try.
- **Sneak Attack:** instead of rolling the extra damage, give the target disadvantage on the save.
- **Monk:** spend 1 Focus Point to give the target disadvantage on the save.

### Scruff

Grab the scruff of the neck. Cats really do go limp when held there. The target makes a Str or Dex save.

- **Fail:** it's Grappled and Incapacitated and can't speak.
- **Breaking free:** at the end of each of its turns it makes a Wis save. On a success it snaps out of it and can yowl, but it stays grappled.
- **While you hold it:** you can Bonk it with advantage or tie it up.

### Pounce

If you move at least 10 ft in a straight line toward the target, or drop 10+ ft onto it, it has disadvantage on the save against your Bonk or Scruff. You take no fall damage because you land on the guard. If the target saves anyway, the noise becomes Loud.

### Valerian drops / Валерьянка

Slip it into food or a drink (it kicks in after 1d4 rounds), or uncork it under the nose of a target you're holding. The target makes a DC 13 Con save.

- **Fail:** Blissed. It's Incapacitated with speed 0 and rolls on the floor purring for 1 minute, then falls Unconscious.
- **Success:** it has disadvantage on Perception for 1 minute.
- **Non-cats aren't affected.** Captain Sevastian is immune.

### Lure

Flick a pebble, wiggle a string or flash a shiny button. The target makes a Wis save against your Takedown DC. On a fail, it counts as unaware of you until the end of your next turn, even if it could see you. The Filter doesn't apply, because it's a physical trick, not a social one.

### Splash

The target makes a Wis save. On a fail, it's Frightened for 1 minute and spends its turns fleeing and shaking itself dry. The guard is out of the fight, but the whole deck hears it. Your own cat has to pass a DC 10 Wis save just to pick up the bucket.

### Truss

A guard that's unconscious or held by Scruff becomes Restrained and can't shout when it wakes. Stuff it in a locker to keep it from being found.

## Story hooks and balance

- **Lunna** can choose to fail her save. A staged takedown and kidnapping is one more way to extract her without blowing her cover.
- **Marty** is inviolable, so every takedown on him fails, comedically. For example, he steps aside to pick up a dropped pencil at exactly the wrong moment.
- **Balance:** the unaware requirement is what keeps these fair, so hold the line on it. Once a fight is open, players use the normal 2024 knockout rule (drop the target to 0 HP with a melee attack).
